University Grading System (NUC 5.00 CGPA Scale)

The University grading system in Nigeria is regulated by the National Universities Commission (NUC). It provides a unified 5-point Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) evaluation metric implemented across Federal, State, and private university degree programs leading to the award of Bachelor's degrees.

Under the standard 5.00 scale, student performance is classified into distinct academic honors bands: First Class Honours (4.50 – 5.00), Second Class Upper Division 2:1 (3.50 – 4.49), Second Class Lower Division 2:2 (2.40 – 3.49), Third Class (1.50 – 2.39), Pass (1.00 – 1.49), and Fail (0.00 – 0.99). These degree classifications strongly influence post-graduate admissions and professional recruitment.
