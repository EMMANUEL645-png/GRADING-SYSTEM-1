Polytechnic Grading System (NBTE Standard)

The Polytechnic grading system in Nigeria is governed by the National Board for Technical Education (NBTE). This standardized framework applies across all Federal, State, and private Polytechnics as well as Colleges of Technology offering National Diploma (ND) and Higher National Diploma (HND) technical education programs.

Academic performance is evaluated on a 4.00 Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) scale. Students achieving a CGPA of 3.50 to 4.00 earn Distinction, while scores between 3.00 and 3.49 receive Upper Credit, and 2.50 to 2.99 obtain Lower Credit. A CGPA of 2.00 to 2.49 results in a Pass grade, whereas any CGPA below 2.00 falls into the Fail category.
