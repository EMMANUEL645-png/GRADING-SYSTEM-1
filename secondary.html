Secondary School Grading System

The Secondary School grading system in Nigeria is the national standard used by examination bodies such as the West African Examinations Council (WAEC), National Examinations Council (NECO), National Business and Technical Examinations Board (NABTEB), and Basic Education Certificate Examination (BECE). It assesses senior and junior secondary students based on continuous assessment tasks and comprehensive final examinations scored on a percentage scale from 0 to 100 marks.

Under this scale, candidate performance is classified into six primary grade letter bands ranging from Grade A down to Grade F. A score of 70% and above represents an Excellent Grade A, while grades C (50-59%) and above are regarded as credit passes necessary for tertiary institution admissions across Nigeria. Scores below 40% are categorized as Grade F (Fail).

Secondary Grading Guide

  • Grade A (Excellent) 70 - 100
  • Grade B (Very Good) 60 - 69
  • Grade C (Credit) 50 - 59
  • Grade D (Pass) 45 - 49
  • Grade E (Fair) 40 - 44
  • Grade F (Fail) 0 - 39
